factory reads this on its tick, turns it into topics and queue items, then you'll
see what it understood in each topic's `continuity.md`.

digest I can actually read Sunday morning with coffee — what mattered, why, and
what's worth a deeper look. Keep it honest: if a week was boring, say so.
